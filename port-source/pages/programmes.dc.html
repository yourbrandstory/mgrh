Alongside the hospital’s day-to-day services, twelve ongoing programmes extend healthcare beyond the hospital and into schools, village centres, community halls, and surrounding villages across Mahabaleshwar.

Some of these are national health programmes implemented locally, including tuberculosis, leprosy, family planning, immunisation, and non-communicable disease control. Others are hospital-led clinics and health camps for mothers, children, school students, and blood donors.

Select any programme below to learn more about the services it provides. All consultations, diagnostic tests, medicines, and hospitalisation related to these programmes are provided free of charge.
