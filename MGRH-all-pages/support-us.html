Support Us Mission

Every consultation, test and medicine here is free of charge. That is possible because of donors, volunteers and partners.

Why we need you

Free for every patient since 1914

Your support helps us continue providing free and quality healthcare to people in Mahabaleshwar and the surrounding rural areas. Your contribution can help us improve hospital facilities, purchase essential medical equipment and medicines, support healthcare camps, and reach more people in need.

Together, we can strengthen rural healthcare and make a lasting difference in our community.

The campus at Mahabaleshwar, Medha Road

Make a Donation

How to give

Contact the hospital office for account details, UPI, or to arrange a donation in kind.

Bank transfer or cheque
Account details are issued by the hospital office on request. Cheques may be drawn in favour of the hospital.
UPI
A UPI ID is available for smaller contributions. Ask the office and a receipt follows by email.
Donation in kind
Equipment, medicines within date, linen and ambulance servicing are all accepted after a short check with the ward in-charge.
Call the hospital office

Not sure how you can help?

Call the hospital office and ask what is needed this month. It may be a donation, a few hours at a camp, or equipment the wards are waiting on.

Call the hospital

Donations to the hospital are eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. A receipt and exemption certificate are issued for every contribution.

WHERE YOUR SUPPORT HELPS

Every contribution goes directly into care. These are the four areas where support makes the biggest difference.

Medical Equipment

Help us buy and maintain monitors, diagnostic machines and theatre equipment so every patient gets accurate, timely care.

Patient Care

Support medicines, consumables and inpatient care for patients from Mahabaleshwar and the surrounding villages.

Infrastructure

Contribute to ward upgrades, clean water, power backup and the upkeep of the hospital building and campus.

Community Health

Fund health camps, screening drives, school clinics and awareness programmes that take care out into rural communities.
