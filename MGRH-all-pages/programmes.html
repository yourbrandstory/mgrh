Our Programmes

Preventive and continuing care for the rural community around Mahabaleshwar, delivered from one campus.

Programmes and community service

12 programmes

Alongside the hospital’s day-to-day services, twelve ongoing programmes extend healthcare beyond the hospital and into schools, village centres, community halls, and surrounding villages across Mahabaleshwar.

Some of these are national health programmes implemented locally, including tuberculosis, leprosy, family planning, immunisation, and non-communicable disease control. Others are hospital-led clinics and health camps for mothers, children, school students, and blood donors.

Select any programme below to learn more about the services it provides. All consultations, diagnostic tests, medicines, and hospitalisation related to these programmes are provided free of charge.

Mother & Child Health

Care for mothers and children from the first antenatal visit through delivery, immunisation and early childhood.

Supervised deliveries with round-the-clock nursing cover. Postnatal care for mother and newborn before discharge.

Antenatal visits through every trimester, with blood tests, sonography and iron supplementation. High-risk pregnancies are flagged early and followed closely.

The full national schedule for infants and children, given on fixed clinic days. Records are maintained so no dose is missed.

Routine check-ups for mothers and young children under one roof. Growth monitoring, nutrition advice and referral when specialist care is needed.

2 programmes

Communicable Disease Control

National control programmes for tuberculosis and leprosy, from early detection to supervised treatment and follow-up.

Sputum testing, diagnosis and supervised treatment under the national programme. Household contacts are traced and screened.

Early detection through skin examination and community case-finding. Multidrug therapy and disability care are followed up locally.

3 programmes

Non-Communicable Disease & Prevention

Screening and continuing care for diabetes, hypertension, heart disease and cancer, with support to stop tobacco use.

Screening and continuing treatment for diabetes, hypertension and heart disease. Medication and follow-up visits at no cost.

Oral, breast and cervical screening camps for the rural population. Suspicious findings are referred onward without delay.

Awareness sessions for schools and the wider community on tobacco harm. Counselling support for those who want to quit.

3 programmes

Community Outreach

Programmes that take care into schools, colleges and villages, including school clinics, blood donation camps and family planning.

Visits to local schools for general check-ups, vision testing and deworming. Children needing treatment are brought to the hospital.

Voluntary donation camps held with local groups and colleges. Collected units support maternity and emergency cases in the region.

Counselling for couples on spacing and contraceptive options. Services and supplies are provided free of charge.
