When you arrive

Bring the patient straight to the casualty entrance. Registration happens after the patient is seen, not before.

1
Come to the casualty entrance
On the near side of the main block, signposted from the campus gate.
2
The duty team sees the patient
Triage happens immediately; the most serious cases are taken first.
3
Tests and treatment follow
Laboratory, X-ray and the theatres are on the same campus, at no cost.
4
Registration afterwards
Paperwork is completed once the patient is stable never before.

Getting to the hospital

Morarji Gokuldas Rural Hospital sits on Mahabaleshwar - Medha Road, set back among the trees, a short drive from the town centre.

From Mahabaleshwar bus stand
Roughly ten minutes by road along Medha Road, heading out of the town centre.
From Satara
About 55 km via the Satara – Mahabaleshwar road; allow around an hour and a half.
By ambulance
Dial 108 for the free state service, or call the hospital and the duty officer will advise.
