Additional Facilities

The hospital campus has doctors and other staff quarters to ensure that medical personnel are available during medical emergencies.

Meals provided for staff and patients typically include breakfast, lunch, and dinner, as well as snacks and beverages throughout the day.

Ambulance services are available round the clock, every day of the week for emergency medical situations.

Autoclave Room dedicated to sterilizing medical equipment, instruments, and supplies. A special device that uses high pressure steam to eliminate bacteria, viruses, fungi, and spores, ensuring that all items are sterile and safe for patient use.
