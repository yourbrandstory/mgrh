Our Medical Team

Meet our resident medical officers and visiting specialists providing clinical care across the hospital's departments.

The hospital is staffed by Resident Medical Officers (RMOs) and specialists, including MD Medicine, Gynaecology. Visiting specialists also conduct clinics on designated days of the week. Nursing and paramedical staff provide round-the-clock care and support in the wards, casualty, and operating theatres.

You do not need to request a specific doctor. Simply register at the OPD counter, and our staff will guide you to the doctor on duty based on your medical needs. If required, you will be referred to the appropriate specialist clinic. Consultation with all doctors listed here is provided free of charge. Diagnostic tests, medicines, and hospitalisation, when required, are also provided free of charge.
