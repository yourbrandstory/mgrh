Who we are

Serving Mahabaleshwar since 1914

Morarji Gokuldas Rural Hospital is situated amidst the lush green landscape of Mahabaleshwar in Satara District, Maharashtra. The hospital building was generously donated by Shri Morarji Gokuldas in 1914 and has been serving the people of Mahabaleshwar and the surrounding villages ever since. Over the years, the hospital has been administered by the Central Government, Zilla Parishad, Municipal Council, and, since 1988, the Government of Maharashtra.

Following a PPP-mode agreement signed in February 2019 between the Health Ministry of the Government of Maharashtra and the Indian Red Cross Society’s Bel-Air Hospital, Panchgani. The Rural Hospital has been renovated and re-equipped by IRCS, Bel-Air Hospital, Panchgani.

Consultations, diagnostic tests, medicines, and inpatient care are provided free of charge under one roof to the local and rural population, as well as to visitors to the region. The hospital is managed by a team of qualified professionals, and the Mahabaleshwar Rural Hospital Managing Committee is headed by Mr. Jayasinh Mariwala, Chairman.
